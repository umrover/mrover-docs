## 2026 - 2027 Projects

### Camera Client Changes

- Easier organization of cameras (ability to move them between windows)
- Ability to change camera settings (exposure, contrast, white-point, and FOV)
- Notification for frozen cameras
- Ability to screenshot all cameras at once
- Ability to tag camera images with site and coordinates
- See the astronaut's video feed

### Auton GUI Changes

#### Astronaut Feed

- Support two way audio-stream with the astronaut
- See health of connection to the astronaut

#### DEM Heatmap

- Add verticality data overlay to current satellite map in basestation

#### Drone Waypoints

- Receive waypoints sent by the drone and let the operator decide how they want to use them

#### Behavioral Tree Visualization

- Ability to see more of the nav behavioral tree when clicking on the nav state
